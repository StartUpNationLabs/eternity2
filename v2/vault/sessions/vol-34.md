## What was attempted

### T1 — vanilla_fast deep-probe with periodic snapshots

Built `--snapshot-dir`, `--snapshot-interval-ms`, `--snapshot-min-depth`,
and a follow-on `--snapshot-on-visit` flag for capturing diverse
partials beyond just the new-max-depth representatives.

30-min × 8-thread probe produced 14 distinct snapshots:
- Max depth = 209 (gate of "≥215" not met)
- Snapshots cluster into 5 distinct basin families
  (intra-thread prefix agreement: 80-90 cells; inter-thread: 0-1)
- 3 of 8 threads got stuck at depth < 200 (62% productive thread
  utilization)

See [[vol-34-basin-clustering]] for the prefix-clustering analysis.

### T2 — unsat-clause-propagator integration

**Encoding reconciliation** SHIPPED:
- `ml/reconcile_unsat_encoding_v2.py`: 23-color bijection via per-color
  frequency-class signature. All 23 colors uniquely matched on first
  iteration. 256/256 our pieces match capiman cards under the color
  map.
- `ml/reconcile_piece_ids.py`: piece_id ↔ card_id + per-pid rotation
  table. Bijective: 256 distinct cards used.

**Hard-pruner integration REFUTED**:
- Validation on vol-32 458 board: 56 conflict pairs against
  forbidden_all.bin, even with correct encoding.
- Round-1-only: still 18 conflicts on the verified-good board.
- Conclusion: capiman's "unsat" clauses are
  **heuristic-search-regime-specific** (per-round, generated by
  capiman's own heuristic+break-index policy). Hard-pruning would
  silently kill valid moves.

**Soft-pruner / value-order PROBED**:
- At depth 200+: signal too weak (good move at 35.7%-ile rank).
- Across depths {30, 50, 80, 120, 160, 200}: rank of ground-truth
  candidate varies 0%-89%. Pattern: strong at shallow depths
  (d≤100), inconsistent at deeper. Vol-35 backlog candidate
  (`unsat-soft-value-order-depth-conditional`).

`ALNS_BUDGET_MS=300_000` × 56 runs ÷ 8 cores = ~35 min wall-clock.

## What was refuted

- **Unsat-clause-propagator as a HARD pruner**: capiman's clauses are
  heuristic-specific, not universally unsat. Silent-correctness risk.
- **Unsat-soft at deep depths (200+)**: too-weak signal.
- **gate-of-depth-215** for T1: max reached 209 in 30 min. The probe
  was killed at that point to start T3 with available partials.

## What was discovered

- **piece_swap_hillclimb bug**: local-delta gain estimator over-counted
  on certain swap topologies; `alns_only`'s log score could be 4
  above the rescore_board's actual score. Affected boards: any from
  alns_only-family bins where polish_swap > 0. Vol-32 records all
  re-verified intact post-fix. Memory entry filed.
- **Basin diversity from 8-thread probe is small**: 5 distinct
  early-prefix families across 14 snapshots. Suggests vol-35 should
  oversubscribe threads (16-32) or use `--snapshot-on-visit` to
  capture intra-basin diversity.
- **Capiman encoding fully reconciled**: future propagator work
  (including non-record-chasing experiments like SAT cooperative
  solvers) can now use the canonical maps without re-deriving them.
