**Why HARMONICS doesn't work as designed**:
The demand graph admits a perfect matching but the matching with
right grid-iso properties (degree + bipartite + 4-cycle + ...) is
*so specific* that no a-priori 2-coloring of pieces accidentally
finds it. The "right" 2-coloring IS the actual puzzle solution's
cell-parity mapping — which is what we're trying to find in the
first place.

**Lesson**: match-first construction requires the right vertex
2-coloring, which is part of the solution. Cannot decouple matching
from layout for this problem.
