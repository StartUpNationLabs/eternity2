## What was measured / kept

- **PARQUET**: sound but **LP capped at 480** on canonical (color balance
  saturates; 2×2 cuts barely move the fractional LP). The base per-edge LP is
  near-tight on small geometric obstructions though — a useful cheap UB oracle.
  [[parquet-overlapping-patch]], [[MATH_NOTES_2026-06-09_PARQUET]].
- **WATERSHED**: deaths are **90% piece-theft** — a dead cell's (N,W) demand has
  1-5 servers, all used elsewhere. Global Hall fires **0 levels early** (deaths
  are local misallocation). **33% of (N,W) color-pairs have a UNIQUE serving
  piece** — E2's combinatorial heart. [[watershed-frontier-flow]].
- **FORGE**: MRV variable-order **HURTS** E2 (fragments the frontier); scarcity
  value-order helps modestly (+4-9 greedy, backtrack-neutral).
- **LATTICE**: **ZERO unconditional forced placements** (single colors have
  20-49 partners; all 196 interior pieces orbit-4; all corners fit all corners).
  Mechanistic root of 15-year unsolvability — no exploitable deterministic local
  structure at any scale. [[lattice-forced-chains]].
- **MOSAIC**: from scratch (no warm start, no ALNS), **447-448/480** (Python
  448, Rust 447, both verified). Hints enforceable as hard pins → **438/480 5/5
  strict-canonical** (user-verified in Bucas). Defects concentrate in the
  bottom-right corner (piece-theft); top ~3/4 near-perfect. [[mosaic-window-maxsat]].
