# Vol-169 — OPHIDIA: Prior-Guided ALNS

Theme: invent a destroy operator that USES the corpus prior. Test escape-vs-attract directions on the 460 plateau.

Status: in progress (sweep launched 2026-05-20 06:54 CEST, ~60min wallclock).

## What was attempted

1. **Math first** (CLAUDE.md depth-over-quick-PoCs): defined cell prior support $s_b(c) = P(b[c], c)$ and destroy distribution $\Pr(\text{seed}=c) \propto \exp(\beta \cdot s_b(c))$. Concept page at [[../concepts/prior-guided-alns]].

2. **Empirical probe** (`scripts/v169_prior_guided/probe_weakness.py`): measured the distribution of $s_b$ across known boards. **Knockout finding**:

| Board | Score | s=0 cells |
|---|---|---|
| V155→ALNS seed1 | 460 | 186/256 |
| V155→ALNS seed13 | 460 | 191/256 |
| V155-direct best_high459 | 460 | 5/256 |
| 462 winning5 s=7 | 462 | 0/256 |
| 469 McGavin | 469 | 0/256 |

   The V155→ALNS 460 boards have 73-75% of cells where the placed piece was NEVER seen at that position in the 49-board corpus. The remaining ~65 supported cells anchor the board to the 460 basin.

3. **Operator design (user-corrected mid-vol)**: user pointed out that "ALNS destroying corpus-supported placements isn't a bug — we're stuck on the 460 corpus basin, we WANT to get out". This flipped the default $\beta$ sign: $\beta > 0$ → destroy strong cells (escape); $\beta < 0$ → destroy weak cells (attract).

4. **Implementation**: `PriorDestroy` in `crates/localsearch/src/alns.rs`. CLI flag `--prior-destroy <path>` on `alns_only`. Appends 3 variants to the existing ops portfolio: `prior_escape_b0.5_k16`, `prior_escape_b2.0_k12`, `prior_attract_b-1.0_k8`. Adaptive ALNS weights pick the right one.

5. **Sweep (in flight)**: 2 base boards × 8 seeds × 30 min = 16 jobs. `basic_lkh` preset + 3 PriorDestroy ops.

## What we'll measure at vol close

- Score lift vs vol-156 round-2 baseline (basic_lkh same bases without prior, also 30min × 8 seeds).
- Op invocation share — does adaptive ALNS prefer escape over attract?
- Variance across 8 seeds (mandatory per CLAUDE.md §4).
- If 461+ found: dump the board, rescore, compute corner-perm + Hamming distance to existing 461 family.

## What was refuted

- The initial design "destroy weak cells (attract)" was incorrect for escaping the 460 plateau. **Reason**: the V155→ALNS 460 boards already have 73-75% weak cells; targeting them more would degrade further, not lift.
