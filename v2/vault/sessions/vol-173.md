# Vol-173 — SPECTRA: Spectral Border Signature Predictor

Theme: train a spectral signature (Fiedler vector of piece-piece compat graph) to predict which border-row configurations lead to 460+ basins.

Status: `partial` — math derived, signature computed, predictor not integrated.

## What was attempted

1. Computed piece-piece compatibility graph $G$ (edge between $p_1, p_2$ if they share an edge color).
2. Computed Fiedler vector $\phi_2$ (eigenvector of 2nd-smallest Laplacian eigenvalue).
3. Hypothesis: border-row sign-pattern of $\phi_2$ correlates with reachability of 460+ ALNS lifts.

## What was measured

- Spectral signature computed for 1278-board DB. Border-row $\phi_2$ sign-patterns cluster but not cleanly by basin family.
- BORDER constant bug discovered (used `0000…` instead of `1111…`). Fixed and re-run.

## What was refuted

- "$\phi_2$ alone distinguishes 460-basin from <460-basin borders" — not refuted, but signal weaker than expected. Not promoted to ranker.
