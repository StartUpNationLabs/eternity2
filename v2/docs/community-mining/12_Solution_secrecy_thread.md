# "Eternity2 information - Disclosure of the known 'solution'" — topic 47714185 (43 msgs, 2011-02)

## The official Tomy disclosure of the solution-generation process

Dave Clark posted in 2007 (msg #914) the full leaflet from TOMY to its
distributors describing how the puzzle was generated and the solution
secured. Re-quoted here in the 2011 thread.

### Key claims about the generation and storage process

1. **Solution-generation hardware was destroyed.** *"To rule out
   hacking, the program that generated the prize puzzle was run on a
   computer whose hard disk and internet connection were disabled. No
   digital record of the solution was kept anywhere. The entire
   computer was destroyed once the solution and the clue puzzle piece
   locations were printed."*

2. **The solution was hidden between random pages.** *"The ETERNITY II
   solution was printed between pages of random text whilst all parties
   were out of the room. Then the solution and all 'junk pages' were
   sealed into an envelope and locked away in the presence of
   witnesses."*

3. **No one alive knows the solution.** *"Neither the inventor nor the
   designers know the solution."* — Monckton, Selby, Riordan all
   forgot the specific board (only the generator algorithm).

4. **Court-revealable, otherwise sealed.** Loss adjusters can produce
   the printout *"as evidence that the puzzle has at least one
   solution. This would be proved if ordered by a court, in which
   event the solution would be shown only to the presiding judge."*

### Implication for the multiple-solutions question

**If E2 has multiple valid full solutions** (per Brendan/McGavin
~14,702 with start piece alone, ~1 expected with 5 hints), the sealed
solution is *one specific instance*. A community-found 480/480 might
not match the sealed one.

McGavin's complex theory estimate (probe #6) for 5-hint canonical is
~4×10^-8 solutions — almost certainly exactly 1 solution. So the
sealed solution **is likely THE solution**, and any community-found
480/480 should match it.

**For the 1-hint variant**, ~14,702 solutions are expected. Blackwood's
470 is on this variant; even if completed to 480, it would be a
*different* board than the sealed one. **No "official" solution
exists for 1-hint or 0-hint variants.**

### Why this matters for vol-9

- Vol-9 should target 5-hint canonical scenario if "matching the
  official sealed solution" is a success criterion.
- Any partial-score above 469 on canonical 5-hint is novel community
  SOTA, regardless of matching the sealed answer.

### Walter Schreur's concern (msg #3)

> *"the possibility that there is an error between the delivered
> pieces to us and the solution which is printed is huge in my
> opinion. In fact they never actually tried the solution with our
> pieces."*

Worth noting: **the generation program was destroyed without
re-verification**. If there's a manufacturing error between the
generator and the printed pieces, the sealed solution might be
unverifiable. But the **mathematical solvability of the printed
piece set** is what matters for community efforts — independent of
whether the original sealed answer matches.
