## What was measured / kept
- **Scarce-demand structure**: 121/362 interior (N,W) demands have a UNIQUE server;
  28 pieces are the unique server of 2–3 demands (57 demands mutually-exclusive);
  64 have a dedicated server. High boards activate ~31 of 121. McGavin↔463 share 24
  (Jaccard 0.73) — BUT corpus-wide (79 boards ≥458) there is **NO ≥80% fixed core**;
  the strong shared-skeleton is refuted. A SOFT attractor remains: demands in ≥50%
  of boards rise monotonically with score (1→2→3→**10** as 420→458).
- **Completability decision**: edge-strict DFS decides perfect-completability in
  <11ms down to **144 placed (7 free rows / 112 cells)**, then EXPLODES at 128
  placed (>1.4M nodes, >3s). Prefix-specific, not universal. [[completability-decision-threshold]]
- **LODESTONE**: at tiny α=0.001 gives median **+2** from-scratch (449→451) AND
  reduces variance (446–451 → 450–451); HURTS at larger α. Real but modest; NOT a
  record-mover. KEYRING+LODESTONE 451 → ALNS basic 5min → 455 (256/256 verified).

## What was refuted / closed
- **TRANSEPT separable construction**: REFUTED. No assignment objective is
  selective — corpus prior vacuous (pieces float: 0/256 row-pinned, 192 float),
  color-balance vacuous (100% of random partitions satisfy the per-stratum matching
  cap). Greedy strip-fill = refuted J1 (perfect rows 0–9, collapse 10–15, plateau
  ~370–385). Residual-steering reduces to exhausted cross-seam ALNS.
- **Sub-480 bound frontier**: CLOSED. (1) Single-window cuts are vacuous (every
  interior window ≤16 cells is internally PERFECT from free piece choice) or unsound
  (boundary-conditioned). (2) Decisive: a **480 solution almost certainly EXISTS**
  (E2 designed solvable), so the TRUE max IS 480 ⇒ **no sound sub-480 bound can
  exist**, and "prove 480 impossible" chases a non-existent object. Published E2
  MILP/clique work confirms no sub-480 bound exists (intractable >8×8).
- **σ-cycle construction (frontier #3)**: not formulable — σ is defined only between
  two KNOWN basins (circular without the target); it collapses into #1.
