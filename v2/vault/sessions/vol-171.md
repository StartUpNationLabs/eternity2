# Vol-171 — MURMURATION: Multi-Basin 460 Sampling

Theme: switch from "lift one 460" to "sample 1000s of 460 basins". User-driven pivot.

Status: in progress (V155 stochastic-beam built; sweep waiting for V169 to finish).

## What was attempted

1. **User pivot**: "there can be 1000s of 460 if we look deep enough, being stuck on one is not really good news". Reframed V169 from refinement to escape — and added V171 as the basin-discovery track.

2. **Diagnosed determinism**: V162/V163 confirmed V155 seed=N only randomizes ties, but at K ≥ 256 there are essentially no ties. **V155 was deterministic in practice across seeds.** No diversity → no basin sampling.

3. **Built V165 STOCHASTIC BEAM as the engine**. Added `--stochastic-temperature T` flag to v155_weaving_prior. Uses Gumbel-top-K: at each depth, perturb each child's combined score by $-\log(-\log U)$ where $U$ is a seeded uniform, then take top-K. Standard sampling-without-replacement-via-Gumbel trick.

4. **Calibrated T**: empirical sweep. T=0.1 → scores 446-453 (mild diversity, decent quality). T=0.5 → 438-441 (real diversity, lower quality). T=2.0 → 240-252 (too hot).

5. **Sweep design**: 48 builds (3 priors × 2 scans × 8 seeds × T=0.1) → 5 min ALNS lift each → cluster by corner_perm.
