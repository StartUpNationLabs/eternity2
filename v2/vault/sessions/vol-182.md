# Vol-182 — ENGRAVE: Exact CSP-Fill on Stripped Row-Bands

Theme: from a known 460 board, strip a row-band of K rows, then run the engine's exact CSP solver to fill the empty cells optimally given remaining pieces and edge constraints.

Status: `partial` — probe done, no record lift.

## What was attempted

1. Take V181 460 board.
2. Strip rows {12, 13, 14, 15} → 64 cells empty + 16 corner/border pieces removed.
3. Run engine's CSP solver constrained by:
   - Corners pinned.
   - Pieces in remaining 192 cells fixed → known edge colors at the strip boundary.
   - Hint pieces still pinned.
4. Solver searches for highest-scoring legal fill.

## What was measured

- Strip K=4 (rows 12–15): solver hits the V129-style row-13 wall at ~15s; best fill recovers original 460 board (proves local rigidity).
- Strip K=2 (rows 14–15): solver finds 32 alternative fills, all score ≤460.
- Compute budget 5min per probe.

## What was refuted

- "Exact CSP on row-band escape produces 461+" — refuted on K∈{2,4} probes.
