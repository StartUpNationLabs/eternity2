# Vol-180 — INTAGLIO-ATTACK: Lex-Ordered ALNS

Theme: ALNS acceptance criterion = lex(score, -forbidden_2x2_count). Use forbidden-2x2 count as secondary objective to break ties on the iso-460 plateau.

Status: `built` — flag wired and used in V181 + 3-basin tests; no record lift, but a sound tie-breaker.

## What was attempted

1. Recall (vol-129): 99.72% of random 4-piece 2x2 patches are forbidden. Real-DB boards: 460+ have ~29 forbidden patches; perfect 480 has 0.
2. ALNS acceptance: accept iff $(\text{score}', -F') >_\text{lex} (\text{score}, -F)$, where $F$ = forbidden-2x2 count.
3. CLI flag `--lex-intaglio` on `alns_only`.

## What was measured

- 3-basin iso-plateau test (cp=(1,0,3,2), cp=(3,0,1,2), cp=(0,3,1,2)) with `--lex-intaglio`:
  - Score 460/458/460 unchanged.
  - Forbidden-2x2 count F shifted slightly (movement on the manifold) but no escape.
- Acceptance rate at SA t=1.0 still ~100% (most moves are score-equal AND F-equal).

## What was refuted

- "Lex acceptance alone breaks the iso-460 plateau" — refuted. Equal-score moves rarely change F either.

## What was kept

- `--lex-intaglio` flag (low cost; useful default for record-track work).
