# Vol-147 — INTAGLIO-pruned DFS

**Theme**: Test the forbidden-2×2 theorem as a DFS pruner. **REFUTED**
within day 1 by inspection + 24-board empirical sanity check.

## Result

Forbidden-2×2 post-placement check on edge-strict DFS is **vacuous**.

### Why

`is_forbidden_2x2(pieces, p_tl, p_tr, p_bl, p_br)` returns True iff
**no rotation assignment** makes all 4 internal edges of the 2×2 match.

In an edge-strict DFS (`vanilla_fast`, `solver-engine`):
- Every placement requires N = upper-S, W = left-E.
- When a 2×2 patch is complete, all 4 internal edges automatically
  match by these constraints.
- Therefore at least ONE rotation assignment (the placed one) makes
  edges match — i.e., the patch is **never** forbidden.

### Empirical sanity check (24 boards, 5400 complete 2×2 patches)

| score regime | n_boards | complete patches | forbidden | forbidden + full-edge-match |
|--------------|----------|------------------|-----------|------------------------------|
| LOW (400-402) | 8 | 1800 | 1789 (99.4%) | 0 |
| MID (441) | 8 | 1800 | 1796 (99.8%) | 0 |
| HIGH (459) | 8 | 1800 | 1788 (99.3%) | 0 |
| **total** | **24** | **5400** | **5373 (99.5%)** | **0** |

Translation: **edge-match implies feasibility** in EVERY observed case.
On real ALNS-final boards (which have mismatches), forbidden patches
abound — but they all have edge mismatches. On edge-strict DFS partials,
no such patches arise.

### What was attempted

- Inspected `vanilla_fast` DFS loop (~85M nps single-thread).
- Realised the post-placement 2×2 check at the BR of the just-placed cell is
  vacuous on edge-strict DFS.
- Considered redesign as forward-check propagator (INTAGLIO-FC) — needs
  column-major or other scan order to be useful.
- Confirmed empirically via `scripts/v147_intaglio_dfs/empirical_sanity.py`.

### What was refuted

- V147 binding item 1 (build patch-check DFS) — refuted in design.
- Binding items 2-3 (measure overhead, canonical run) — never executed.

### What was kept

- `scripts/v147_intaglio_dfs/empirical_sanity.py` — useful tool for
  future analysis (stratified-by-score forbidden-2×2 counter).
- Concept page [[../concepts/intaglio-pruned-dfs]] updated to `refuted`
  with the rotation-vs-edge-match analysis.

### What's still open

The forbidden-2×2 theorem's prune power exists, but at the **piece-supply
level**, not the DFS placement level. Possible follow-ons:

1. **Piece-supply LP cut**: incorporate forbidden-piece-set bounds in
   per-color LP relaxation. Likely subsumed by vol-44 cell-pair LP.
2. **ALNS-init pruning**: ALNS allows edge mismatches in the partial
   state. A "no forbidden 2×2 in the current partial" filter could
   reject acceptance of bad swaps. But V140/143 already tried this as
   ALNS tiebreak; inert.
3. **Column-major DFS with INTAGLIO-FC**: forward-check non-trivial
   in column-major. Cost analysis suggests overhead > prune benefit.
   Defer.
