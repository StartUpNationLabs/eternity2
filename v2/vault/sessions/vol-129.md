# Vol-129 — PALIMPSEST: Historical Consensus

**Theme**: Mine the 1278-record DB for adjacencies that persist
across runs. Use persistence as a signal of "correct" structure.
Targets HIGH-consensus low-score regions as the *consensus traps*
to break.

**Per user binding** [[feedback_e2_one_invention_per_volume]]:
vol-129 is PALIMPSEST-only.

## Why now

CONCORD (vol-126) `partial`, CONCRETION (vol-127) `refuted`, ATLAS
(vol-128) `refuted`. The pure-static-analysis approaches all bottomed
out. PALIMPSEST uses our ACCUMULATED EXPERIMENTAL DATA — the 1278-board
record DB — as its substrate. Much more E2-specific.

V125-T34 (FPL) is a primitive form of PALIMPSEST: per-pair log-odds
across high/mid/low score buckets. PALIMPSEST goes further by:

1. **Adjacency consensus**: not just per-pair but per-adjacency
   (cell-pair, piece-pair) freq → strong consensus where many high
   boards agree.
2. **Consensus-trap detection**: regions of 99% inter-board agreement
   that ARE NOT in any 470+ board. The brainstorm calls this the
   "consensus is the wrong cluster" insight. Force destroy in those
   regions.
3. **Score-weighted aggregation**: boards near 461 weight more than
   boards near 440. Compute per-pair `freq = sum(score * indicator)
   / sum(score)`.

## Binding items (3 max)

1. **Consensus map**: per-(cell-pair, piece-pair, rotation) compute
   score-weighted persistence. Identify (a) high-persistence pairs in
   any 460+ board, (b) high-persistence pairs that NEVER appear in
   462+ boards.
2. **Consensus-trap targeting**: build a "deliberately-destroy-this"
   list — the high-persistence-low-score-ceiling pairs.
3. **ALNS with consensus-aware destroy**: extend `alns_only` with a
   destroy operator that picks positions whose current pair is in the
   trap list. Run multi-seed.
