## Open at close

All three STRATUM variants refuted in 1 vol. Pattern repeated from V147:
edge-strict DFS pre-satisfies "already-placed" invariants by
construction, so any propagator over already-placed state is vacuous.

**Universal lesson** (V147 + V149):
> In edge-strict row-major DFS, the placed cells are *always*
> simultaneously feasible against constraints involving only those
> placed cells. Pruners that look at "what is" do not fire.
> Useful pruners must look at "what could be" — i.e., forward-check
> over candidates for unplaced cells, OR involve mismatches (which
> strict DFS doesn't create).

This rules out a broad class of "static partial-board invariant"
inventions. Future inventions in this autonomous arc should target:
- **Forward-check propagators** that look at *unplaced* cell candidates.
- **ALNS-internal** propagators (ALNS allows mismatches, so X_c > 0 fires).
- **Variable-order** changes (different DFS scan order — V149-v2 tried,
  worse-not-better because spiral order lost NW-bucket).
- **Genuinely new search architectures** (CHIASMUS coupled DFS+ALNS,
  WEAVING co-evolutionary, OUTRIDER template-based).
