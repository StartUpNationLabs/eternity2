## Open at close

**BRAID self-refuted via design analysis** in 2 hours.

Drafted three Python PoCs:
- `braid_poc.py`: row-major DFS with weft-state tracking → identical
  to vanilla_fast.
- `braid_color_first.py`: branch on (S, E) colors per cell instead
  of pieces → larger branching factor, no genuine advantage.
- `braid_warp_beam.py`: row-shaped beam units → equivalent to V151
  cell-level beam.

**The realization** (mid-vol):
> Framing matters less than search primitive. BRAID's "warp threads"
> and V151's "row prefix in beam state" describe the same search
> with different vocabulary. Renaming a concept does not invent a
> new algorithm.

## Lesson

After V147, V149, V152, and now V153: many "new inventions" reduce
to variants of constrained DFS with different branching units or
heuristics. This is the comfort-lottery anti-pattern documented in
CLAUDE.md.

**Genuinely-different search primitives** that remain untried:
- DLX (Knuth Algorithm X) — different data structure, faster on exact
  cover.
- RL self-play with neural value approximation — different objective.
- Wang-Landau MCMC over the lattice gas — different sampling strategy.
- Quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) with quantum-
  inspired solvers — different optimization regime.
