# Eulerian-cycle border propagator (anr_56 2007)

**Status**: `refuted` (vol-9, zero pruning on canonical E2)
**Origin**: anr_56 groups.io 2007 (Eulerian theorem application)
**Files**: `crates/propagators/src/border_eulerian.rs`

## The check

A valid border ring on E2 must trace an Eulerian cycle in the **edge-color graph** of border pieces: vertices = border-edge colors, edges = pieces with that color pair. The ring is closeable iff every vertex has even degree and the graph is connected.

## Refutation (vol-9)

Tested on canonical E2 at 5 depths × 200k trials each = 1M:
- **Zero pruning power.** The Eulerian condition is satisfied trivially by all border subsets generated by standard search.
- The constraint is **necessary but vacuous** on the actual canonical piece set.

## Why it fails

The Selby-Riordan generator structure (rare colors only on the border ring, 2 per border piece) makes Eulerian closure automatic for any border subset that gets close to a full ring. The check fires only on configurations that fail much-earlier propagators (gacolor, AC-3).

## What this taught us

Vol-9 was the first systematic port of a community technique. The lesson: **measure with falsifiable A/B before believing community lore**. Subsequent ports (vol-12 NS-1, vol-15 Blackwood) all carried explicit pruning-power measurements.
