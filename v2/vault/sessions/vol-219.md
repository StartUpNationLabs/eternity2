### ★★ STAGE-3 IS INSTANTLY EXHAUSTIBLE (caught ~22:20 UTC, after user interrupt)

The bulk run "finished" in ~30 min instead of 5 h. Investigation
(triggered by the user's machine-load warning) found `elapsed_ms: 2`
against `budget_ms: 30000` in the run logs. First hypothesis — a
`--thread-id-offset` hint-shuffle bug — was REFUTED by direct
evidence (hint pool lives outside the shuffled bucket region;
empirical check: clue pieces correct at 34/45/135 in t01/t04/t05
states and in offset snapshots). The truth is structural:

**Every stage-3 subtree under a floor-0 stage-2 state EXHAUSTS
instantly.** All 3,822 vol-217 "30 s" banking runs: elapsed min/med/
p99/max = 0/0/5/18 ms; placements min/med/p99/max =
28/10,551/231k/1.1M. Same state at offset 0 and offset 1 explores
exactly 9,463 placements (exhaustive node counts are
order-invariant). The "30 s budget" never bound — the banked
snapshots were a throttled SAMPLE (the `--snapshot-interval-ms 300`
gate plus a now_ms==0 quirk banks only the first-millisecond
visits) of a tree that was already fully traversed.

Consequences (vol-217's "exhaustible-and-bad" is now quantified and
operational):

1. **Producer states explained exactly**: a stage-2 state either
   contains d≥192 leaves in its ~10k-node subtree or it doesn't
   (144/3,822 = 3.8% do). No luck involved — order-invariant.
2. **Offset diversity is VACUOUS at this tier** (my own morning plan
   refuted same-day): different offsets re-traverse the same
   exhausted tree in a different order. The bulk run's snapshots are
   samples of the same complete leaf sets.
3. **The right collector is exhaustive enumeration**: bank every
   transition into depth 192 (each is a distinct rows-0-11
   completion). Cost ≈ ms/state + process startup — the ENTIRE
   current stage-2 corpus (23,784 snapshots, not just the 3,822
   floor-0) can be exhausted in ~minutes with a batched driver.
4. **The fb_oracle floor-0 passage filter is OBSOLETE in this
   pipeline**: it costs ~1 s/state to predict what exhaustion
   answers exactly in ~5 ms. Don't predict what you can measure —
   the vol-218 conclusion eating its own pipeline stage.
5. **The supply bottleneck moves UP a tier**: entry supply scales
   only with fresh stage-2 generation (from-scratch walks ARE
   non-exhaustible). Overnight plan: stage-2 gen × hours →
   dedup → exhaust-collect → label.
6. **Binding 3 (steered row-11 generation) is OBSOLETED at its
   registered tier**: candidate-order steering inside an exhaustible
   tree cannot beat having ALL leaves. Steering only means anything
   at non-exhaustible tiers (stage-2 and below) — that is tier-2
   ASSAYER territory, logged in BACKLOG, not this vol's binding.
   CURRENT-VOL to be amended per the discovery-obsoletes-binding
