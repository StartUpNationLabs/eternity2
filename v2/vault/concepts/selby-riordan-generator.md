# Selby-Riordan generator (canonical E2 piece set)

**Status**: external (the actual generator behind the official puzzle)
**Origin**: vol-7 rediscovery, vol-10 measurement
**Reference**: Selby & Riordan 2000, archduke.org

## Statement

The 256 pieces of canonical Eternity II were generated by Selby & Riordan using a procedure with two key properties:

1. **[[rare-color-rule|Rare-opposite-edge rule]]**: rare colors (1–5) never appear adjacent within a piece; only on opposite edges.
2. **Flatness**: the piece histogram has minimal structure. Vol-10 PCA: top-1 PC ratio 0.0856 (vs iid baseline 0.0455). Top-3 PCs explain only 25% of variance. Effectively designed to defeat static spectral analysis.

## Implications measured across volumes

- **Vol-7**: the rare-opposite rule is the ONE generator-level structural finding our team discovered that isn't in the 26-year community corpus (vol-8 mining).
- **Vol-10**: spectral/geometric invariants ruled out. PCA, graph Laplacian, position-aware pairwise — all bounded. The generator was tuned to flatten these.
- **Vol-10 / vol-13**: per-piece combinatorial structure DOES carry signal: pieces 17/38/62 have unique forced placements; rare colors live only on the border ring (see [[rare-color-rule]]).
- **Vol-17 R4 (MI measurement)**: interior-only adjacent piece-side MI = 0.27 bits / 6.6% reduction. The Selby-Riordan generator successfully decorrelated interior sides.

## What the generator does NOT do

- Doesn't avoid all structure: piece-uniqueness binding for ~10¹⁰¹× overcounting in tensor methods (vol-13). NS-1 multiset-equality invariant (vol-11) is necessary.
- Doesn't ensure unique solution: McGavin's complex theory estimates ~4×10⁻⁸ expected solutions on canonical 5-clue — *probably* exactly one exists.

## Why this matters for solver design

- **Don't bet on global statistical structure** (PCA, BP marginals, MPS marginals). The generator removed it.
- **Do exploit per-piece exact structure** (pieces 17/38/62, rare-color border exclusivity).
- **Do exploit hint-induced strain** (the 5 canonical hints create asymmetry the generator didn't compensate for; see [[mismatch-geometry]]).
