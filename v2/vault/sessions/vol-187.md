## What was attempted

1. **INTAGLIO-MIP** (`intaglio_mip.py`): exact MIP for joint row-band swap. Variables: x[v, p, r] + match-color indicators. Constraints: cell-uniqueness, piece-uniqueness, soft external-edge matching via objective. Solver: HiGHS.

4. **1h ALNS budget extension** on V181 460 base (V186-T2 carryover).

## Implications

V181 460 is structurally locked at the 30-cell-region granularity. To reach 461+ requires either:

1. **A region larger than what MIP can prove rigid** (40+ cells) — and the LP-UB shows slack there.
2. **Non-rectangular regions** — diagonal, L-shape, or scattered.
3. **Cross-basin transport** — taking pieces from a higher-score basin's geometry.
4. **A different basin entirely** — restart search from scratch with V181/V175 builder variants.

The next vol should pick angle 3 or 4 — angles 1 and 2 are local-search variants and likely face the same MIP-convergence wall.
