# Vol-124 — W-SAT validated + 24×3 corner-perm sweep refutation

## Theme

Validate the W-SAT toolchain (post vol-123 encoder bug fix) and then
use it to systematically screen the corner-permutation × border-DP
enumeration space.

## Raw

(no archive; inline-session vol).

## What was attempted

1. **SAT correctness round-trip** on 5 puzzle sizes (4×4 through 7×7,
   colors 4 and 6) using `sat_e2 → kissat → sat_decode_to_board →
   verify_partial.py`.
2. **Auto-sabotage UNSAT tests** per user prompt: 4-BORDER-edge piece on
   interior cell + duplicate-piece (piece-uniqueness violation).
3. **Border-only single-rotation sabotage**: take a valid 5×5 border,
   rotate ONE corner by 90°, re-encode. Compare SAT vs UNSAT.
4. **W11 corner-perm sweep**: enumerate all 24 4-corner permutations ×
   up to 3 chain solutions each = 72 border-DP partials. SAT-screen each.

## What was measured / kept

- Round-trip on 4×4–7×7: **all SAT**, all decoded solutions verified as
  complete with full edge-matching.
- 4-BORDER-edge sabotage: **UNSAT 0.01s** ✓ (encoder catches physical
  impossibility).
- Duplicate-piece sabotage: **UNSAT 0.18s** ✓ (alldiff enforced).
- Border-only 1-rotation sabotage on 5×5: **UNSAT 0.00s** (control: SAT 0.02s).
  A single 90° rotation flips the decision — exactly what W11 needs.
- **W11 sweep (chains=3)**: 72 borders × kissat-5s. Result: **71 UNSAT + 1 ENCODE_FAILED
  (re-ran manually: also UNSAT). 0 SAT. 0 TIMEOUT.**
- **W11 wider sweep (chains=10)**: 240 borders × kissat-5s. Result:
  **240 UNSAT, 0 SAT, 0 TIMEOUT, 0 ENCODE_FAILED** in 7.5 min.
- Aggregate: **312/312 border-DP corner-perm partials are UNSAT for 480**
  across two enumeration depths (chains-per-side ∈ {3, 10}). The right
  480 border is NOT in this class.

## What was refuted

- **The right 480 border for canonical E2 is NOT in the border-DP class
  with our current chain-enumeration depth** (first-3-chains per side).
- All 24 corner permutations explored; all 3 chain-solutions tested each.
- The border-DP search must either (a) be expanded (more chain solutions
  per side: `--max-chains-per-side > 3`), (b) use a different border
  generator (e.g., not greedy chain-DP), or (c) the conclusion stands —
  the 480 border has structure outside the border-DP polytope.

## Re-evaluation: SAT-screen-per-border is NOT a viable solver

User correctly observed: "there are billions of possible puzzle borders".
Quick math:
- Border space lower bound: ~10^7 - 10^9 distinct chain-matched borders
  on canonical E2.
- Our 240 borders screened in 7.5 min = 1.9s/border.
- 10^7 × 1.9s = ~22 years of single-thread SAT time.
- Even 1000-core cloud parallelism = 8 days. Not feasible.

**Conclusion: W11 (SAT-screen per-border) cannot be the primary engine
on canonical E2.** It works as a *filter inside* a smarter outer search
(e.g., "given a candidate from algorithm X, can it extend to 480?"), but
NOT as an enumeration driver.

The whole-puzzle SAT encoding (border-pieces as variables, no external
enumeration) is the only feasible direction. That's what PID 72166 is
running — but kissat at 30 min has no decision. The path forward is
**tightening the SAT encoding** (better symmetry breaking, smarter
preprocessors, learned lemmas from W7 backbone) rather than enumerating
borders.

## What carries to vol-125

1. **W11 is RETIRED as primary engine** but kept as a verification tool
   (post-hoc check on borders from other algorithms).
2. **Tightening canonical-E2 SAT encoding**:
   - Symmetry breaking on the 24 corner permutations (4 corners can be
     placed in only 1 of 24 perms without changing the puzzle —
     `S_4`-quotient = 6 distinct corners). Saves ~factor 4 search.
   - Symmetry breaking on the 4-fold board rotations (additional factor 4).
   - Per-piece-rotation choice for corner pieces is forced by their
     2-border edges — should be encoded as units (already done).
   - Add **LP-derived clauses**: from the LP-UB-478 polytope, infer pairs
     of cells (i, j) where matched(i) AND matched(j) is incompatible.
     Add as binary clauses to kissat instance.
3. **Different solvers**: try CaDiCaL, MiniSAT, MapleCOMSPS in addition
   to kissat. Different CDCL heuristics may have different progress
   curves on this instance.
