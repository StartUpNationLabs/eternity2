# Vol-177 — NEURONIC: Tiny NN Ranker for Beam Search

Theme: train a small learned model to predict "is this partial board on a 460+ trajectory?" and use as beam ranker.

Status: `partial` — models trained, R² insufficient to justify integration. GBM superseded by V181 patch+pheromone signal.

## What was attempted

1. Feature extraction: per-board (matched_edges_so_far, depth, corner_perm, border_density, prior_sum, pheromone_sum).
2. Models tried:
   - **MLPRegressor**: R² = **-9** (overfit catastrophically).
   - **GBM (GradientBoostingRegressor)**: R² = **0.40** (modest predictive power).
3. Plan to integrate as additive score in beam ranking.

## What was measured

| Model | R² | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| OLS | 0.30 | baseline |
| MLPRegressor | -9 | overfit |
| GBM (depth=4, n=200) | 0.40 | modest gain |

## What was refuted

- "MLP can learn the prior/board → 460-trajectory map" — refuted on this dataset size.

## What was kept

- GBM training pipeline. Could be revived with 10× more training data.
