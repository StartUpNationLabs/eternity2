# Vol-172 — CHIASMUS: Cross-Basin Row Interleave

Theme: take corpus boards from different basin families, interleave their row-bands, lift with ALNS. Test whether cross-basin crossover can escape iso-basin ALNS plateaus.

Status: **partial** — implemented; the "461" results are σ-related-basin artefacts, not novel breakthroughs.

## What was attempted

1. Selected 18 ≥458 boards from DB grouped by corner-perm (cp).
2. Built 56 hybrids: row-interleaves of pairs (top8A/mid8A/border4A/rowAB patterns).
3. ALNS 5min × 56 hybrids.

## What was measured

- Top 4 hybrids returned **score 461** with 0 piece conflicts on input.
- All 4 are cp=(1,2,3,0) × cp=(1,2,0,3) interleaves.
- ALNS contribution: **0**. `best_score_history = 1` entry; iter=0 already at 461.

Diagnosis: cp1230 and cp1203 share enough pieces in their halves that a row-interleave gives a valid 461 board already in the DB. They are σ-related basins.

Other 52 hybrids (different cp pairs) scored 371–436; ALNS could not repair conflicts in 5min.

## What was refuted

- "CHIASMUS crossover yields a new 461" — refuted. The 461 already existed in DB; CHIASMUS just shuffled it.

## What was kept

- **Basin-proximity discovery**: cp=(1,2,3,0) and cp=(1,2,0,3) are σ-near. Future cross-basin work should target only such near-pairs.
- Hybrid generator at `scripts/v172_chiasmus/build_hybrids.py`.
