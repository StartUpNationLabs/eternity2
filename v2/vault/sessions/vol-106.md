## What was shipped (T1)

`crates/blackwood-fast` — new workspace crate. ~480 LOC. Three
iterations:

1. **Initial port** (40-56M nps): Vec-based state, single flat
   candidate index, try-time border filter.
2. **Bitset + 4-table** (60-65M nps): u64 bitset for pieces_used,
   4 border-aware candidate tables, bottom_of/right_of LUTs.
3. **Sentinel + unsafe** (65-68M nps): sentinel-terminated lists
   (libblackwood trick), `get_unchecked` on the sized hot path.

Const-generic specialisation `solve_raw_sized<256, 256, 4>` for
canonical 16×16 with stack-allocated state.

## End-to-end pipeline measurement (T13, post-T12)

Pipeline: PGO+unrolled `bf_bw` → ALNS post-fill on canonical
Selby-Riordan 16×16.

| stage                         | result          | wall  |
|-------------------------------|-----------------|-------|
| bf_bw 4t × 5min (depth=245)   | score 444       | 5 min |
| + ALNS 2min (seed=1)          | score 450       | 7 min |
| + ALNS 2min (seed=7)          | score 451       | 7 min |
| + ALNS 2min (seed=42)         | score 451       | 7 min |

Stable 450-451 across 3 seeds (variance ~0.5%, per CLAUDE.md rule #4).

vs. comparable measurements:
- vol-15 calibrated_v17a + ALNS: 447 at 5min   (we +3-4)
- vol-32 cold-start:              458 in hours  (we -7-8)
- vol-60 standing:                459 in days   (we -8-9)

**Read**: the +25% single-thread nps from T12 unrolling does NOT
translate to score improvement at multi-thread 5min budget — the
search converges on the same trajectory. The real value of T12 is
faster single-thread variant for cold-start sweeps + per-thread
budget reduction.

4-thread vs 8-thread on apple-m1: 4t gives 52M nps/thread, 8t
gives 22M/thread (memory contention). Same final score (444 at
5min); 4t uses fewer cores for same outcome.
