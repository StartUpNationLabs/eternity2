## What was shipped (T1)

`crates/blackwood-fast` — new workspace crate. ~480 LOC. Three
iterations:

1. **Initial port** (40-56M nps): Vec-based state, single flat
   candidate index, try-time border filter.
2. **Bitset + 4-table** (60-65M nps): u64 bitset for pieces_used,
   4 border-aware candidate tables, bottom_of/right_of LUTs.
3. **Sentinel + unsafe** (65-68M nps): sentinel-terminated lists
   (libblackwood trick), `get_unchecked` on the sized hot path.

Const-generic specialisation `solve_raw_sized<256, 256, 4>` for
canonical 16×16 with stack-allocated state.
