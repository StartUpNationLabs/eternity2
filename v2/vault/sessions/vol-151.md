**Findings:**
- Beam-search lifts from-scratch ceiling V150→V151: **408→455 (+47)**.
- U-shaped K curve with used-mask dedup; path-dedup needed at high K.
- Single rollout as ranking signal is too noisy; **rollout averaged across N replicas** is the unbuilt extension.
- Score saturates around 455-460 even at K=16384.
- Time scaling: K=16384 in ~21 min wallclock single-thread. Memory ~10MB.

**vs records:**
- Strict-canonical 459: -4 score gap from a from-scratch builder. Possibly closeable.
- Matched-edges 463 (V129-T12): -8 gap.
- Community 469 (McGavin): -14 gap.

**Open at close:**
