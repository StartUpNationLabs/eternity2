# Vol-179 — LARGE-K: Bigger Destroy Regions in PriorDestroy

Theme: extend V169 OPHIDIA PriorDestroy with larger k (32, 48) to allow basin-spanning escapes.

Status: `built` — variants integrated; tested as part of 3-basin iso-plateau analysis.

## What was attempted

1. Add variants `prior_huge_b1.0_k32` and `prior_huge_b4.0_k48` to PriorDestroy op portfolio.
2. Rationale: V169 max k was 16; if escape needs to disturb 30+ cells coherently, k=16 is too small.

## What was measured

- Combined with V180 INTAGLIO-ATTACK lex on 3 distinct ≥458 basins.
- Across 1100–1300 destroy+repair iterations per basin:
  - All ops invoked heavily including k=48.
  - **0 cell diff vs base after 30min**.
- Op invocation share: adaptive ALNS picked LARGE-K variants ~10% (broadly comparable to small-k variants).

## What was refuted

- "k=48 destroy is enough to escape local 460 basins" — refuted on 3 basins.
