   must be perfectly matched. McGavin 469 (11 mismatches) → Z=0 past row 1. Condition

2. **Apparent result (REFUTED)**: at χ=6, free-interior n_eff dropped 749→19 at K=8 —
   a "40× sharpening", with shuffle-control (→Z=0) and 9.4% predictive top-1 (19×
   chance). Looked like a real tail-resolution lever.
3. **★ χ-convergence check KILLED it**: at FIXED K=8, n_eff = 1.9 / 2.4 / 19 / 243 for
   χ = 2/4/6/8 — monotonically CLIMBING, not converged, heading back toward the
   unconditional ~749. **The sharpening is a low-χ TRUNCATION ARTIFACT** (pinning
   creates long-range entanglement the truncated MPS drops, artificially concentrating
   the marginal). Conditional contractions need χ ≫ tractable at K=23.
4. **Verdict**: conditional marginals via χ-truncated MPS are **NOT a usable tool for
   E2's tail**. The constructor (binding item 2) was NOT built — it would steer by
   noise (anti-pattern). [[tidemark-conditional-marginals]] = refuted-as-tool.
