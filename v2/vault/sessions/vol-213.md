## Day 2 (2026-06-10 evening) — REPLAY mode (`--prior-over-cost`)

Built per the day-1 "open (a)" item. The priors machinery ordered ONLY
cost-0 candidates (break segments stayed lazy in shuffle order), so the
witness walk forked into a cost-0 subtree at its first break cell
(depth 171/167) instead of paying the witness's break — that is exactly
the 455-457 saturation. REPLAY: when `break_open`, drain ALL open
segments into the per-depth buffer and sort by **(Reverse(weight),
cost)** — a weight-1 break candidate now beats unseen cost-0
candidates. Soundness: `break_open` is invariant within a cell instance
(any backtrack through depth d resets its cursor to FRESH), so the
eager all-segment fill at first visit enumerates exactly the legal
candidate set. Plain-priors path unchanged (nfill=1, stable sort,
zero break-bits ⇒ bit-identical ordering).

Expected mechanics on witness A (gates 171-195, et14 trigger 182):
straight walk to 171 (perfect prefix, weight-1 cost-0), breaks paid at
171/171/174/177/177/179/181 (7 pre-trigger), exact tail absorbs row 13
(witness pays 13 there) ⇒ FIRST completion should be ≈20 breaks = 460;
anytime backtracking then explores the perfect-prefix neighborhood
(depths ~170-181 first) with exact endgames, hunting ≤19 = 461+.

Tests: `prior_over_cost_replays_pool_solution` (pool = synthetic
solution ⇒ straight walk, <100 nodes, 0 breaks, epoch 1) and
`prior_over_cost_break_accounting` (foreign pool; in-DFS recount
asserts guard the buffer's cost-1 accounting). 20/20 green.

Runs launched: both witnesses, 8 seeds × 300 s, exact day-1 config + the
flag (controls: A 455/456/457, B 456×8).

### REPLAY first result + ★★ the DOUBLE-BREAK discovery

Witness-A REPLAY (300 s × 8): **458/458/458 — all 8 seeds, II 352 +

★★ **Structural fact**: our break-DFS candidate machinery generates
violate-EXACTLY-ONE segments only — a free cell can never pay 2 breaks
at once. The community strict-460s contain 4-5 double-break cells ⇒
they are **unreachable boards for our DFS under ANY budget, gates,
priors, or scan order**. This explains (a) the witness-guided

what), and possibly (b) part of the unguided 450 plateau — the
violate-≤1-per-cell space is a strict subspace of legal boards, and
the high-total boards may systematically need double-break cells
(2 wrongs at one cell can buy 3+ rights later; the witnesses
demonstrate exactly this trade).

Fix in flight: cost-2 segments (violate exactly 2 of k≥2 constraints,
gated as two breaks: `spent+2+hint_after ≤ budget` and
`d ≥ sched[spent+1]`), flag `--max-cell-breaks 2`; REPLAY buffer
