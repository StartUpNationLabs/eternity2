column-by-column, tracks (top piece, bot piece, used-set) per beam state.

- **Per-band**: bands 0..7 achieve perfect 46/46 score at beam=100k.
- **Chain over all bands** (beam=100k): first complete J1 board — **444/480 matched, 0/5 hints**.
- **Forward-Look Heuristic** (FLH): weight final-band beam by future
  piece-supply compat → **447/480** (+3).
- **Loss localization** ($\sum H = 14 \cdot 15 = 210$ proved by counting):
  all 36 missing edges are in $V_8..V_{14}$ + bottom-row horizontals.
  Inner-row horizontals and $V_0..V_7$ are PERFECT.

  in principle reach $E_{\text{lo}}^{\text{internal}}=232$ (= 480 board).
  Empirical: greedy column-DP reproduces J1+FLH (447), 8-cell FLH-weight
  sweep all return 447. Bound stands; greedy doesn't reach it.
